The Briefing

Alan Cumming · your host

A long table laid for dinner in the great hall: crystal, candles, and eight places. Halfway through the second course a wheel is pushed in on a trolley and set at the head of the table.

Alan Cumming taps a glass with a knife until the room is quiet.

You have won thirty-eight thousand pounds. Tonight you can win more of it, and you can lose what you have.

Three spins. Before each one, you decide together how much of the pot goes on the table: raise your glass to stake, cover it to hold. The majority carries.

Alan Cumming sets the ball in the rim and does not spin it yet.

If the ball lands on the castle's colour, your stake comes back doubled. If it does not, it stays with me.

One pocket is green. It pays no money at all — whoever staked the most that spin takes a Shield instead.

And everyone will know who wanted the big bet.

Eat first. Then we spin.
