    The off-season
    The months between one season airing and the next. Resolving it works out what happened to everybody in that time — who got together, who broke up, who moved, who fell out — and lists it below as proposals. It rolls; it does not decide. Nothing here is true yet.
    Canon
    What the site treats as having actually happened. Committing saves this list to the franchise, and from then on the approved rows are the ones that appear on wiki pages, on Dramagram and in a character's history. Rejected and undecided rows stay here and are shown to nobody.

    Two things ignore this entirely and always wait for you: anything irreversible — a death, leaving the franchise — and anything that contradicts what you have written in a backstory.
