The Traitors · Night 10 · The Last Fire

THE FIRE OF TRUTH

One word each, folded and fed to the flames. Every hand at this fire has to agree before it goes out — and nobody who leaves from here is ever turned over.

I. The Fire

Nobody Is Going To Be Turned Over

The fire is built for a castle that used to hold twenty. Four of them sit down at it and are not asked to accuse anybody. They are asked something else.

One word each, folded and fed to the flames. It takes the whole fire to put it out, and one hand to keep it lit — so the quietest player here holds exactly the power the loudest one does, and neither has to explain it.
