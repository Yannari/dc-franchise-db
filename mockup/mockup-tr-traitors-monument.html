The Briefing

Alan Cumming · your host

A moor at dusk. A carved stone monument, taller than anyone, covered in a code of lines and dots. Six hooded figures in rust-red robes stand around it, each with a symbol stitched on the chest. Beyond them, a sword in a boulder.

Alan Cumming lays a gloved hand on the monument.

This stone is written in the Traitors' own code. Read it, and it will tell you which of these figures to go to.

Alan Cumming walks slowly round the ring of hooded figures.

Each of them will give you a riddle. Some want the stone turned. One will ask you to name everyone the Traitors have taken, in order.

Read the stone wrong, or answer wrong, and that figure turns its back on you for good.

Every riddle you answer puts money in the pot.

When you are done, one of you may draw the Sacred Sword. Whoever does can take a Shield.

But if they take it, everything you won here today stays in the stone.

You have until the light goes. Begin.
