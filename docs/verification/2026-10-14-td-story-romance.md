# TD: deeper romance, and none when it's off — 2026-10-14

The user's plan, step 3 (2026-10-12: "competing priorities, vulnerability, awkward disagreements, earned
reconciliation"), and 2026-10-14: "don't forget romance".

## Measured first (the story-step-3 matrix)

- With romance **on**, a couple had no romance scene in 522 of its 739 episodes on screen, one in 194, two in 23.
  The engine's romance moments are mostly flirts (73 of 265), and nothing carried a couple from one episode to the next
  apart from the follow-ups.
- With romance **off**, 152 romance scenes aired anyway: the engine's flirtation and showmance moments never checked the
  switch, spin the bottle played with a "friends" ending, and the long night-game scenes are romantic.

## What changed

- **Off means off.** With romance off, flirtations and showmance moments don't happen, spin the bottle is truth or dare
  (the same draw, so nothing else in the season moves), and no romance scene is written out long (camp-events.js,
  director.js longScene).
- **A couple between their engine moments** (`td/story/couple.js`, `lines/n-couple.js`): one beat a couple a stretch of
  the day, by what is true for them now:
  - *priority*: a tribal night where their ballots name different people: one asks for the other's vote ("If I asked
    you to switch to Izzy, would you?" / "Then I don't know. Give me until the vote.");
  - *disagree*: one of them doesn't trust somebody the other is friends with; awkward, then quiet; recorded;
  - *mend*: one to three episodes later, with that person still there: they make it right, and the scene says who it
    was about ("I still don't trust Bowie. But I trust you, and you trust Bowie, so I'll keep my mouth shut.");
  - *open*: every third episode or so, something real ("I'm scared I'm only still here because of you");
  - *daily*: otherwise, a small moment of their own.
  Each moves their bond (priority and disagree down a little, mend and open up), and what a beat starts is recorded
  only if it airs. Priority and disagree are the day's story, mend a payoff that is due, daily colour.
- From the read: the island's "replaying the vote" scene said "four votes" whatever the count was; it says "that many
  votes" now, and the pool has four more (the island repetition guard had tripped on the new seasons).
