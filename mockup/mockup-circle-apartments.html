The Circle: 12 apartments (Blender, stylized 3D)

Each apartment is its own place: a beach shack, an arcade den, a glam suite, a boho nest, a sports den, a music room, a library, an artist's studio, a space room, a diner, a brick loft and a zen room. Apartment N gets room N, so up to 12 players on at once never share a room. A newcomer who moves into a blocked player's apartment takes over that room. Each room has two shots: the door closed (waiting, and the TV during chats) and the door open with the couch (a visit). The overlays below sit on positions measured from the camera, not eyeballed.
