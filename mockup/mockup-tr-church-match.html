The Briefing

Alan Cumming · your host

A candlelit church. At the back, a double confessional with a grille on each side. In the pews, twelve silent figures in black lace masks. On a lectern, the Book of Traitors.

Alan Cumming sits down in the middle of the double confessional, between the two grilles.

One of each team sits in here with me. Through this grille I will give you a page and a paragraph in the Book of Traitors.

Somewhere in that paragraph is a riddle, and the riddle describes something one of those masked people is wearing. The rest of your team goes into the pews and finds it.

Alan Cumming nods toward the silent congregation.

You may look. You may not touch. Touch a mask, or name the wrong one, and that riddle is lost to you.

Every item you name correctly is two thousand pounds for the pot.

The team that names the most will kneel at the altar, and choose one of its own to receive a Shield.

Only one of you. The rest of you will have to watch who your team chose.

Six riddles each. The candles are lit. Begin.
