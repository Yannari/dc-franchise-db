# Secret Story France — research notes (all 14 seasons, 2007–2026)

Sources: fr.wikipedia "Secret Story" + "Saison N de Secret Story" (1–14, raw wikitext in this folder),
en.wikipedia "Secret Story (French TV series) season N" (1–13), press (ozap, viralmag, orange) for S12–14.
No season 15 exists yet (S14 aired June 2026).

## The constant core (every season)
- House of Secrets, cut off from the world; the oven clock is reset every night so they lose the time.
- **La Voix** (male, Dominique Duforest, all 14 seasons; S12 faked his retirement with a female voice — that WAS the house's shared secret "we live in the fake house"). Game master: rules, missions, punishments, dilemmas, speaks any time, they must freeze and be silent.
- **Ten commandments** (S1–9, S12–14): never reveal your secret; never reveal your mission; stop when the Voice speaks; obey without contesting; never interrupt; call it "la Voix"; only talk to it from the confessional; never refuse a mission; justify nominations honestly; never discuss nominations (S1–7); never reveal your vote (revival). Breaking one → penalty: no hot water, money taken, **nominated d'office** (automatic nomination), worst = disqualified.
- **Kitty (cagnotte)**: €10,000 each at the start. Winner's cheque €150k (S1–7), €100k (S8+), plus own kitty.
- **The secret**: personal ("I survived the tsunami", "I'm a medium", "I have 100 tattoos") or *production-made* ("we are a fake couple", "we are the intruders", "I am the Voice's accomplice"). Shared secrets are common (couples, twins, family, fake couples, fake siblings).
- **Buzz**: red buzzer in the confessional → confrontation with the Voice present, target defends without revealing → accuser can withdraw or confirm, staking **€5,000**. Answer revealed later to the whole house in the living room. Right: takes the target's whole kitty (half if only half the secret). Wrong: loses the stake (to the target). Need ≥€5,000 to buzz (S11: Voice left Barbara at €4,999 as a joke). Free buzzes are a reward.
- Half-secret rule: finding part of a secret ("works for the police" of "police officer AND stripper") wins half the kitty.
- Revealing your own secret (even privately, even "heavy clues") → kitty wiped + nominated d'office (S2: disqualified). Telling your secret partner's secret also counts.
- **Secret hunt ends at the semi-final**; surviving secrets are revealed then. S12+: keeping your secret to the semi DOUBLES your kitty.
- **Nominations**: in the confessional, the Voice sets which sex is nominated, the other sex nominates two each; alternate weekly. Sometimes "mixed" (everyone nominates, still opposite sex) or fully open. Top 2 vote-getters nominated (ties → 3–4, or a second round). Plus automatic nominees (d'office) from rule breaks and powers.
- **The public votes to SAVE**; lowest % leaves, in the **sas** (airlock between house and studio) live with the host. Final = public vote among 4 finalists (S2: 6 for 4 with double eviction at semi).
- **The sas**: nominees wait there for the verdict; also where the house meets eliminated players / family behind a glass wall.
- **Confessional**: daily confessions, nominations, buzzing, talking to the Voice.
- **The prime** (weekly live show, Friday later Thursday; none in S12 except launch/final): reveals, dilemmas, red phone, eliminations, family in the sas, video of what others said about you.
- Final week: all evicted come back for 24h each to support the finalists (from S3). Families visit late season.

## Recurring mechanics (and where)
| mechanic | what it is | seasons |
|---|---|---|
| Red phone (téléphone rouge) | rings during prime or week; whoever answers gets good/bad news: money ±, extra vote against you, nominate d'office someone, immunity, a dilemma, even an elimination (S4 Julie, S13 Dréa) | 2,3,4,5,6,7,8,10,11,13 |
| Black phone | the Voice's threat line ("your adventure is compromised") | 4,6 |
| Secret room / pièce secrète | fake eviction: public votes, the "evicted" hide and watch the house on screens, return later, often with nominating power | 1,2,3,5,6,7,8,9,11,13 |
| Second house | a parallel house: intruders, antechamber, Secret Box, Hotel, Crystal House, Yacht | 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10,11,13,14 |
| Immunity house vs nomination house | everyone split; whoever is in the nomination house at the deadline is nominated | 5,6,7 |
| Nomination à vie | a player nominated every week until gone | 5,11 |
| Masters of nominations | a secret pair adds a nominee each week until discovered (S7); or a weekly winner nominates directly (S12 "Master of the House") | 5,7,9,10,12 |
| Joker / switch card | swap a nominee for someone else | 4,5,6,7,10,13 |
| Inverted nomination card | the nominated are immune and vice versa | 5,6 |
| Fake immunity card | you hand an "immunity" that is really a nomination | 8,10,11 |
| Bridge/Garden of sacrifices (pont/jardin des sacrifices) | a clue about your secret goes public unless you sacrifice: money, a nomination, someone's visit, an immunity | 5,10,11,13 |
| Clues | objects (a fern, a leopard scarf, a "J" cube, a cassette with a song, a USB key), words ("Serrure", "Nuit"), subliminal videos, the clue dispenser | all |
| Room of truths / lie detector / thermal camera | test sincerity; watch what others said about you | 1,5,6,10,11 |
| Secret of the house | a riddle hidden in the house all season; solving it = a place in the final (S4 "une place en finale", S6 the word FINALE from numbers in every room) | 3,4,5,6,7,13 |
| Ticket / place in the final or semi | won, then a dilemma: give it away for money | 3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10 |
| Pooled kitty | all kitties merged; group choices raise or lower it; clan kitties | 4,5,6,11 |
| Dilemmas | almost every prime: money vs seeing family, save a friend vs keep your clue, etc. | all |
| Fake couple / fake siblings / fake ex mission | the Voice's big mission; win money if the house believes it until it's revealed | 2,4,5,6,9,10,11,13 |
| Seduction / jealousy missions | "seduce X to test Y's jealousy", "pretend to be in love", "fake break-up", fake wedding (S4 Amélie & Senna, with a S2 priest) | 4,5,6,10,11 |
| Secret missions for money | €2k–€30k | all |
| Supérette | timed shop: 90 seconds to grab the week's food | 1–4, 10, 11 |
| Love Room | a couples' bedroom, a reward | 5,6 |
| Returning alumni | as guests with powers (S13 rings: immunity, nomination, switch, jackpot swap) or as secret-holders (S9 Vanessa "I'm a former candidate") | 4,6,7,8,9,10,11,13 |
| Clans | the house splits into rival clans by S5+; clan names, clan chiefs, clan kitties | 5,6,9,10,11 |
| Miss / Mister Secret, Secrets d'or ceremony | house elections and awards for fun | 6,10,11 |
| Exclusion | the Voice disqualifies for violence/threats; the house sometimes votes on the return | 2,3,4,6,8,10 |

## Season by season (the signature of each)
1 (2007) — 10 wks. Triplets playing as one (won). "I'm the Voice's accomplice" (Gabriel, never found). Room of truths with lie detector. First fake eviction (Maryline & Xavier in a secret bedroom). Married couple secret.
2 (2008) — Mother-daughter secret blurted → mother disqualified, daughter kept by public vote 85%. Fake couple Matthias/Alice (won). Red phone debut (double vote). Anglican priest excluded for telling his job. Secret room = immunity.
3 (2009) — Intruders' house reached by an underground passage. Miss France in disguise for 48h (blew it by saying her real name). A dog with a secret. Violent player excluded by HOUSE vote. Fake nominations; "secret of the house" = place in final. Former players return 24h in final week (becomes tradition). Winner's secret: "my worst enemy is in the house".
4 (2010) — 15 wks. Before Secret (web vote picks a fake couple: "the internet's favourite fake couple" — won, biggest prize €197,805). Antechamber: 3 "sacrificed" watch the house and must identify who sacrificed them. Fake pregnancy mission. Joker card. Pooled kitty. Second house. Fake wedding with S2's priest. Duos nominations. Semi-final dilemmas (€15k each).
5 (2011) — Underground labyrinth: twins "masters of the undergrounds" spy and pick a player NOMINATED FOR LIFE. Crystal ball on the roof (public picks who enters). Couples swapped by the Voice. Red phones x2 (answer before the 5th ring or everyone is nominated). Love Room. Immunity house vs nomination house. Instant-elimination power (fake: to a secret room, then she nominates live). "I am the key to the secret of the house" (his tattoo was the answer).
6 (2012) — 15 wks. Secret Box: 3 watch from a tinted cube in the living room, must find one secret to take that person's place. Fake siblings Nadège/Thomas (linked: shared nomination and kitty; won with 73%). Library of secrets with power books (Wishes, Ruin, Investigator, Immunity). Thermal camera. A fake new housemate "Marc" who is really the public. Clans (blue room / pink room). Pooled kitty. Secret room pair chosen by public, nominate live. Semi: secret of the house = the word FINALE.
7 (2013) — The number 7, an elevator to 7 secret rooms, 7 mysteries. "We are the family of the public" (4 entered early). "Masters of the nominations" (add a nominee weekly until discovered). "I am the common secret of all the housemates" (nobody found it → all nominated). First alumni guests with powers.
8 (2014) — Rooftop chalet (AB studios). 4 "impostors" each playing two identities (8 people from 4). Each prime themed ("the house drops the mask", "plays with fire", "travels in time"). Fake mother/son (Nathalie & Vivian). Room of shields (fake immunity). Two ejected at once after a fight.
9 (2015) — Small second house with 6 trapdoors into the main one. Former S3 player hidden under a new name (found in < 1 week). "Subjects of a love experiment". Voice's adventurers (Dubai). Finale cancelled (Paris attacks); winner announced by video.
10 (2016) — 10th anniversary: Secret Boat before-show (3 past winners pick 2). Hotel of secrets next door; portrait hunt among 200 portraits. "Mistresses of identities" (replace one voter's nominations). First pregnant man. "I am the alibi of the couple". Temptation trials, switch cards, safe with everyone's kitty (Fanny + a secret ally take it all if undiscovered). Clan wars, gender war, supérette back. Fake ex played by an actress.
11 (2017) — Campus of secrets (American university + Harry Potter magic). Control tower with secret doors (watch all cameras). "Neighbours" infiltrated (outed by a host's on-air slip). Married couple + a forced fake couple. Best friend sacrificed herself at the door. New students arrive mid-season knowing everyone's secrets (must protect them). Nominated for life. Forest of mysteries / well of truths. Pie-in-the-face race for a room key.
12 (2024) — Revival, 8 weeks, NO weekly prime (evictions in the Friday daily). Fake house + fake female Voice on night 1 (the shared secret). 200 m² game arena. Weekly "Master of the House" challenge: immune + nominates one directly; housemates nominate two. "We are the house thieves" (start at €0, steal from kitties, find hidden coins). "We are not twins". "I entered the house on day 2" (an actress played her on day 1). Some evictions BY housemates (power). Keeping your secret to the semi doubles your kitty. Winner: first individual-secret winner.
13 (2025) — Prime back (Thursday). Crystal House (second house; the main house must guess how many live there). "Guardians of transparency" (spy on both houses, may publish videos). Linked pair (one nominated → both). Fake couple. Red phone eviction. Jungle of decisions. Alumni with rings (immunity, nomination, switch, jackpot swap). Bridge of sacrifices: clue or +2 votes against you. Secret of the house riddle → place in final.
14 (2026) — Yacht of secrets vs house (two clans who can watch each other). "Creator of the avatars" (8 fake identities to stop the yacht learning who lives in the house), "the oracle of the house", "protector of the couple". Confidential files (photos, childhood, social profiles) handed across. A finalist reached the final with €0 (also S12).

## The look (reference list for later plates)
- Logo: an EYE (John Matos artwork), 2007–2017 dark/neon; 2024 colourful gradient eye.
- Classic house (S1–7): prefab on a car park at La Plaine Saint-Denis, re-themed each year (designer Michèle Sarfati). Rooms: living room (high-tech, pool table, flat TV), American kitchen (the oven clock), girls' and boys' bedrooms, confessional with the red BUZZER and a hatch, bathroom (rationed water), dressings, laundry/réserve, the SAS with a glass wall, garden with pool (10.5 × 5.5 m), outdoor kitchen, pergola with long table, the supérette, red carpet to the studio. Mona Lisa pictures hidden around (S1). Secret passages, trapdoors, hidden doors everywhere.
- Rooftop chalet (S8–11): wooden chalet 20 m up on the AB studios roof, 3 levels (+ basement), 1000 m² garden, floating pool, jacuzzi, bedrooms upstairs with shutters, view over Paris, 64 cameras behind mirrors. S11 = campus decor.
- Revival house (S12–14): warehouse at Poissy, 1000 m², 73 cameras, 200 m² game arena, 40 m² pool shaped like a giant rectangular washbasin, a vertical sliding wall that splits the house in two; studio set beside the house.
- Signature props: the buzzer, the red phone (Ericofon), the black phone, the secret board/screen listing the secrets, the clue dispenser, cassette/USB clues, cards (joker, switch, immunity), envelopes, the library of secret books, the lie detector, the thermal camera, the crystal ball, the Secret Box cube, the eye.
- Music: "I Wanna Chat" (Booty Full) title song; the Voice's intro line: "Ils ont tous un secret… méfiez-vous des apparences."
