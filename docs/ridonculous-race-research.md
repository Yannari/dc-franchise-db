# The Ridonculous Race + The Amazing Race: research notes

Read episode by episode before writing the design (user, 2026-10-18: "analyze ridonculous race and amazing race
thoroughly, many episodes… I don't want you to leave anything possible out, it's a 1:1 almost").

Sources: Total Drama wiki episode pages (Plot, Route Markers, Leaderboard, Trivia), the wiki's gameplay-elements,
challenges and injuries lists, Wikiquote for the spoken lines; The Amazing Race wiki for the rules and real legs.
No full transcripts of the Ridonculous Race exist online (checked the TD wiki, the Transcripts wiki, subtitle sites).

Each episode note keeps what a simulator needs: **the route as segments**, **how order and time gaps are made and
undone**, **what teams do to each other**, **what goes wrong**, **how the last team is found**, and **the story
beats** that carried the episode.

## Part 1: The Ridonculous Race, episode by episode

### The rules (gameplay elements page)

- **Don Box** = clue box, dispenses a **Travel Tip** (blue/black card) from its mouth. Themed per country sometimes
  (Don hates that; likes the Argentine one). A **Travel Tip giver** (a local) can hand tips instead.
- **Interview** = confessional: a dark room with a world map, or on location, and always at elimination.
- **Chill Zone** = Pit Stop, a gold round carpet (Carpet of Completion). Don announces placements. A penalised team
  waits beside it until the clock runs out. Last team in "may or may not" be cut. Rewards are rare and small (first
  class, food, a call home), unlike the Amazing Race's cars and trips.
- **All-In** = both members must do it, or a penalty. The most common task; one or two per leg.
- **Either/Or** = Detour. Two named tasks ("Feast or Fossil"), switching allowed. Only five in the season, all but one
  on a non-elimination leg.
- **Botch or Watch** = Roadblock. One member, the other watches and may not help. **Must alternate**: doing two in a row
  is a penalty (the Rockers nearly did). **Random Botch**: the tip decides who (the one who did / didn't pull the tip).
- **Penalty clock**: not following the tip, unauthorised travel, skipping an All-In, helping on a Botch or Watch, two
  Botches in a row, flagrant cheating. **Ten minutes to an hour, Don's call.** Served standing beside the Chill Zone.
  The Ice Dancers were penalised the most (three times, all late in the season, for cheating).
- **Boomerang** = U-Turn-ish: forces a team to **redo the previous challenge**. Randomly on the back of a tip at the
  first Don Box of a country; usable until the next Don Box, then it expires. Several per leg possible. Can be used on
  yourself (Stephanie on Ryan). Each team that got one was also hit by one that same leg.
- **Superteam** = Intersection: several teams must work together (three teams noodle six catfish).
- **Zip-It Ticket** = Fast Forward: skip the rest, straight to the Chill Zone. Only once, earned inside a Superteam,
  three-way tie for first.
- **Golden Travel Tip** in the finale: the map to the finish. **Finale has two Chill Zones**: a bronze midpoint one
  (last team there is out, third place) and the final one.

### Ep 1 · None Down, Eighteen to Go, Part 1 (Toronto) · non-elimination

- **Opening**: all teams arrive at a train station in waves (bus, train, taxi, a dramatic rope entrance); Don
  introduces each team in one or two lines while they arrive. Intros carry the whole season's set-ups: Carrie's secret
  crush, Devin's unseen girlfriend, Taylor's ingratitude, Emma doubting Kitty, the Stepbrothers forced to race by
  their newly married parents, Noah wanting to finally win something, the LARPers believing in magic.
- **Rules speech**: 26 legs, Chill Zone each leg, last team *may* be cut, first to the final Chill Zone wins a million.
- **Route**: on foot to the tower and the first Don Box → **Either/Or** Stairs (144 flights) / Scares (elevator +
  skywalk outside the tower) → zip line over the lake to the island airport, grab a tip in mid-air → **book a flight;
  three flights, six teams each**, assigned by arrival order.
- **Bottleneck**: the elevator. Dwayne presses every button trying to high-five Junior, so several teams switch to the
  stairs; the elevator teams queue at the bottom and **talk while they wait** (Owen and Leonard). The second elevator
  load is gassed by Owen.
- **Mishaps that cost time**: altitude sickness (Mickey), a sibling fight on the stairs, a partner carried (MacArthur
  carries Sanders; Geoff carries Brody with a cramp), a janitor throws his tools at the Fashion Bloggers after an
  insult, the Tennis Rivals knock each other off the zip line into the lake.
- **Refusals**: the Fashion Bloggers won't wear the helmet (hair). A refusal is a choice that costs.
- **Partner moments**: Carrie calms Devin's fear of heights; Owen saves Noah from the wind. Danger is where bonds move.
- **No placement**: the "result" of the leg is **which flight** you made. That is the order the next leg starts in.

### Ep 2 · Part 2 (Morocco desert) · LARPers out

- **The flights are the structure**: flight 1 lands and races while flight 2 is in the air while flight 3 waits.
  **Cut between the three groups.** The third flight's six teams wait at the airport and try to make alliances (the
  LARPers repel everyone).
- **Route**: All-In at a spice kiosk (pick the five right spices; knowledge helps, others guess at random under
  pressure) → camel ride to a desert restaurant → **Botch or Watch**: eat a stew made with *your own* spices (a bad
  pick earlier costs you here: tasks chain) → on foot to the Chill Zone.
- **A team's quirk becomes a choice**: the Vegans refuse to ride their camel (walk beside it), start a campaign.
- **Mishaps**: a camel collapses (Father & Son), a sprained ankle (Carrie, carried by Devin to first place), cramps
  (Sanders carried), temporary blindness from spice (Dwayne dragged by Junior), crashing into a tree while chasing.
- **Rule broken without a penalty**: MacArthur pours the stew into Sanders (helping on a Botch). The wiki flags it as a
  goof; for us it is a judgement call that can go either way.
- **Bottom of the leg is a race of three**: the last three teams are close; the LARPers try to "use magic" to slow
  the other two; both pass them. **Sabotage that fails is the elimination.**
- **Eliminated team's exit**: Don's one line, then the team's own interview looking on the bright side.
- **Interview room tantrum**: Josée after third place. Interviews carry the feeling the race hides.

### Ep 3 · French is an Eiffel Language (Paris) · Tennis Rivals out

- **Leg start**: teams **depart in the order they arrived** (mopeds, staggered). Order matters again at the airport
  (two flights).
- **Contact between teams on the road**: the Cadets overtake the BFFs; the Ice Dancers overtake the Cadets; the
  Fashion Bloggers accidentally throw a carpet onto Father & Son (slows them); the Cadets **ram the Ice Dancers' cab**
  to get ahead. Rivalry Cadets × Ice Dancers starts here and runs all season.
- **Route**: cab to the tower → **Botch or Watch** (whoever did not eat the stew): draw a caricature of your partner,
  approved by an artist (the drawings are character comedy: Emma drawn with horns, Chet as a garbage bag) →
  catacomb maze, find the exit and a tip among cheese wheels (MacArthur's sense of smell) → **ride the cheese wheel down
  the river** → carry it to the Louvre.
- **Cab luck**: a flat tyre (the Fashion Bloggers go shopping during it), a driver who doesn't know where to go after
  the team fell asleep (Tennis Rivals fall far behind).
- **Following**: the Tennis Rivals follow the Vegans, assuming they know the way; both are misled by Owen's smell.
- **First penalty**: Owen ate the cheese raft → 20 minutes. **The penalty decides the elimination**: the Reality TV Pros'
  clock runs out just as the Tennis Rivals arrive; both dive for the mat; Gerry's foot is not on it; the Rivals are out.
  The **closest finish of the season is staged as a dive onto the carpet.**
- **Wrong method costs time**: the Rivals carried their cheese instead of rolling it.

### Ep 4 · Mediterranean Homesick Blues (south of France → Iceland) · Geniuses out

- **Leaving early is caught**: the Ice Dancers try to depart before the first-place Cadets; Don and the Cadets scold
  them. MacArthur now watches them.
- **Route**: train to the coast (teams bunch at the station; trains carry several teams) → **Botch or Watch**: swim in
  shark water and take the tip off a shark's dorsal fin → All-In: build a sand replica of a palace, judged by a pro →
  speedboat to the next country's Chill Zone.
- **Ways around a task, by character**: the Geniuses use a magnet (and are cut off for being boring in the interview);
  Crimson scares a shark into giving up its tip; Brody, the confident swimmer, is chased the whole time (skill ≠
  success when the task fights back).
- **Getting a cab**: MacArthur uses her badge to commandeer one. A mime steals the BFFs' tip and Devin chases him.
  Junior falls for Carrie; Dwayne ruins his attempt to talk to her.
- **Unauthorised travel**: the Fashion Bloggers skip the train and arrive on a prince's yacht → penalty at the mat.
  Father & Son read the tip before reaching the shore → penalty, which hands first place to the Ice Dancers.
  **Two penalties at one Chill Zone reshuffle the top.**
- **Planning loses**: the Geniuses over-plan the sandcastle, a wave washes the plans away, they can't build without
  them. **Don flies out by helicopter to eliminate them where they are**: a team too far behind never reaches the mat.
- **Equipment breaks**: the BFFs' and Surfers' boats break down; Brody holds the wires and is electrocuted all the
  way. The Surfers think they're last and accept it; Don tells them they're not. **A false-last moment.**

### Ep 5 · Bjorken Telephone (Iceland) · non-elimination (Vegans last)

- **Departure order is contested**: the Ice Dancers race to leave first, five teams arrive right behind them, everyone
  argues; **buses carry several teams** so the lead evaporates. Then **helicopters in waves of six**.
- **Route**: All-In: hear Don say a phrase, run across a geyser field, repeat it to a local girl (get it wrong → run
  back and hear it again) → helicopter → **Either/Or**: Feast (petrified shark, sheep head…) / Fossil (pick a fossil
  out of an ice-cave wall, slide it out).
- **An identity is a key to a task**: the Goths learned the language from their favourite bands; Jen gets the tip by
  complimenting the local's outfit without saying the phrase. **People-skill shortcuts exist.**
- **Partner dynamics play out at the task**: Taylor sends her mother back alone to re-hear the phrase (→ one-hour
  penalty at the mat: both must go). Stephanie yells at Ryan. Emma won't let Kitty help, Kitty does anyway, Emma
  scolds her; at the fossil Kitty keeps her word and lets Emma push alone. Junior covers Dwayne's mouth and says the
  phrase himself. **Who leads the pair shifts during a leg.**
- **Switching an Either/Or**: Mother & Daughter switch to Feast, then switch straight back after watching Dwayne nearly
  impale himself. **Watching other teams drives choices.**
- **The Goths pick the task nobody else is doing.** Contrarian choice as character.
- **Theft**: Dwayne takes the Vegans' fossil (Junior feels it's wrong). At the mat Don **refuses the stolen fossil** and
  the Vegans must start again; they end up eating the feast against their principles. **A sabotage gets caught at the
  Chill Zone, and the victim pays more than the thief.** Laurie's rage at Dwayne becomes a grudge.
- **Last team, non-elimination**: Laurie, having betrayed her principles for nothing, attacks Don. Non-elimination
  reveals are their own scene.
- Holdover injuries carry between legs (Brody still paralysed from last leg's electrocution; Geoff carries and feeds him).

### Ep 6 · Brazilian Pain Forest (Brazil) · Vegans out

- **Flights split by placement**: first eight on a direct flight, last seven on a flight two hours later with animals.
  **An accident flips it**: Dwayne's broken fossil bursts the first plane's tyre, delaying it. The equaliser is luck.
- **The second flight lands first and races alone for a while**, then the first flight arrives and the Ice Dancers
  storm through. **Order on the ground is not the order of the flights.**
- **Route**: **Botch or Watch**: pull a tip from a mitten full of bullet ants → cross a gorge on vines, search coconut
  piles for a tip → All-In: make a carnival headdress + tail costume → **hang-glide from a cliff to the Chill Zone**.
- **Small kindnesses make alliances**: Kitty defends Mickey in the ant queue; Mickey hands her an extra tip; Kitty
  pushes Emma into a **trial alliance** with the Twins; they **crash into each other gliding in, tie for 7th/8th**, and
  dissolve the alliance on the spot.
- **Accidents between teams**: Mickey pushes Laurie face-first into the ants; Taylor throws a coconut at swollen
  Laurie and knocks her off the cliff (the coconut cracks and gives the Vegans their tip). **Accidents can help.**
- **Teammate conflict**: MacArthur won't let injured Sanders help; Sanders finds the tip anyway with a mallet.
- **Penalty decides it again**: the Vegans arrive first but Miles sewed both pieces of an All-In costume (30 min); they
  end up last and out. **Doing an All-In alone is a penalty.**
- **Delayed effects**: Spud's delayed pain reaction makes him faster at the end.

### Ep 7 · A Tisket, a Casket, I'm Gonna Blow a Gasket (Romania) · Fashion Bloggers out

- **A lead wiped out by shared transport**: the Ice Dancers leave first, then learn everyone takes the same bus. At the
  airport **Stephanie has Ryan throw her over the queue** to get the first-flight tickets.
- **Physical sabotage**: Josée pushes Sanders down a hill; MacArthur tackles the Ice Dancers; a fight. Both teams fall
  behind **because of the feud** (sabotage costs the saboteur too).
- **Route**: All-In at the castle → whoever pulls the tip **drags their partner in a coffin** to the graveyard →
  horseback to a gym → **Botch or Watch** (the one who didn't do the ants): balance beam + vault, judged by a local
  coach → on foot to the Chill Zone.
- **Breaking a rule on purpose for character**: the Goths swap places in the coffin to both experience it, knowing it's
  a penalty (10 min); they don't care. **A team can value something over position.**
- **Old wounds**: Josée pushes the coffin hard as revenge for Jacques dropping her at the Olympics. **Backstory
  surfaces under pressure.**
- **Team in-fight about their identity**: the Fashion Bloggers argue over who founded the blog and fall behind; they
  reconcile after Tom is electrocuted. **The last two teams race for the mat**; the Bloggers lose but are glad they raced
  together.
- **Help across teams offered and refused**: Geoff suggests the Stepbrothers work together; Lorenzo refuses.
- Skills from life: Chet did rhythmic gymnastics at school.

### Ep 8 · Hawaiian Honeyruin (Hawaii) · non-elimination (Daters last)

- **Waiting together makes character scenes**: donkey carts shared by teams, charades (Kitty/Owen/Emma/Noah); Dwayne and
  Junior share a cart with the Stepbrothers; Taylor tells Junior he's lucky to race with his dad.
- **Route**: **Botch or Watch** (the one who didn't do gymnastics): dive for a wedding ring in a honeymoon bay, swim it
  to your partner → All-In: walk hot coals carrying your partner, one in a grass skirt, one in a lei; if either catches
  fire, start over → on foot to the Chill Zone.
- **Competition over the same object**: Stephanie and Kitty fight for one ring; a shark interrupts; Kitty escapes with
  it. **Noah finds two rings and gives the spare to Mickey** (out of pity; he insists he's not soft).
- **Cultural rule broken**: Josée takes a lava rock as a lucky charm despite a local warning her. It's cursed and costs
  them for three legs (the Goths reveal it in ep 11). **A small choice becomes a season arc.**
- **Romance machinery**: Kitty's "love radar" spots Carrie's crush; Kitty coaches Carrie; Noah falls for Emma; Owen asks
  Kitty what's wrong with Noah. **Kitty is the matchmaker hub.**
- **Embarrassment decides who does what**: Junior won't wear the skirt (classmates); MacArthur forces Sanders into it;
  Crimson won't until she colours it black (costs time).
- **A family's truth comes out in a fight**: Kelly tells Taylor her trophies were bought. Big emotional beat mid-task.
- **Breakup at the mat**: Ryan dumps Stephanie when they're last; Don cuts in: non-elimination. **Last place is where
  relationships break.**
- Geoff distracts Brody from the pain by talking about cold things: **teamwork as one partner managing the other.**

### Ep 9 · Hello and Dubai (Dubai) · Mother & Daughter out

- **Rewards**: last leg's winners fly **first class** (jealousy from Jacques).
- **Flights as social rooms**: Kitty pitches an alliance with the Reality TV Pros (Emma refuses); Kelly sits next to
  Dwayne, he pitches an alliance, then insults Taylor. Owen and Kitty agree to **set up Noah and Emma**.
- **Route**: bus → **Either/Or** (the only one on an elimination leg): Serve (return a ball from a tennis launcher atop
  the tower) / Squeegee (wash a column of windows top to bottom from a platform) → on foot to the Chill Zone in a gold
  market.
- **Watching others decides the choice, again**: flight 2 all pick Serve after seeing the Sisters nearly die on
  Squeegee; then several switch after seeing the launcher hurt people. The Twins stick with Serve because the squeegee
  **sound** bothers them. **Identity-level quirks drive task choice.**
- **One team's chaos hits another's task**: Stephanie knocks the launcher off the building onto the Sisters' platform;
  Emma falls; **Noah catches her**, attraction follows, and an **alliance is formed in the moment**. Owen uses his body
  as a squeegee and finishes for both teams. The broken launcher makes Serve easier for everyone after.
- **Alliance teams hand each other placement**: Noah lets the Sisters onto the mat first.
- **Forgetting to check in**: Mother & Daughter reach the gold market and go shopping before stepping on the mat; the BFFs
  pass them; they're out. **The mat is a step you can miss.** Kelly ends glad, and cancels Taylor's allowance.

### Ep 10 · New Beijinging (Beijing) · non-elimination (Rockers last)

- **Plane chats**: Carrie listens to Devin talk in his sleep; Rock (wanting Devin's peanuts) accidentally tells her to
  confess. Rock tries strategy talk; Spud's answer is "party harder". **The plane is where a team's strategy is talked.**
- **Route**: All-In: find the stadium and **tandem-skydive into it** (wind blows some off course) → All-In: one partner
  skewers exotic animals, the other eats them deep-fried → **Botch or Watch** (the one who didn't dive): pull your partner
  by rickshaw to the Great Wall → find the Chill Zone on the Wall (which way?).
- **Bribes as teamwork**: Rock bribes Spud with snacks; Junior uses his dad's obliviousness. **Managing your partner.**
- **Switching who does an All-In role** mid-task: Carrie and Devin swap; Carrie eats with Devin cheering her on.
- **A deal for help**: the Sisters' rickshaw breaks; **Noah and Owen share theirs if Emma agrees to an alliance**; she
  does. **Help is traded for alliance.**
- **Wrong way at the end**: the Ice Dancers use the lucky rock to pick a direction and go wrong; the Cadets and Surfers
  follow them wrong too. **Direction choice on the last stretch reshuffles.**
- **Temporary alliance from attraction**: Brody likes MacArthur, so the Cadets and Surfers team up.
- Ryan hits every bump pulling Stephanie (pettiness as a time cost); then Ryan refuses Stephanie's direction, carries
  her his way, and **wins the leg**. Arguing about direction is a scene with a result.
- **Wrong person on a Botch** (Spud instead of Rock) would have been a penalty; last place on a non-elim covers it.

### Ep 11 · I Love Ridonc & Roll (Finland) · Twins out

- **Sharing a cab**: Spud flags one and gives the Daters, Goths and Cadets a ride; Rock pitches an alliance (rejected);
  Spud reveals he can't refuse a dare. **MacArthur files that away and uses it later**: dares him to kiss a seal, it
  attacks him, the Cadets pass them. **Information from a casual chat becomes sabotage.**
- **Route**: All-In: sit in a sauna at max heat for ten minutes fully clothed (two teams per sauna) → cross a
  half-frozen river → **Botch or Watch**: air guitar in front of a live crowd until an appeal meter fills → Chill Zone.
- **Capacity bottleneck**: two teams per sauna. Noah **waits on purpose** for the Sisters (to be with Emma) and drops
  from first to seventh. **Romance costs places.**
- **Identity revealed**: the Goths' makeup melts and washes off; they've never seen each other "ungothed"; they hide in
  paper bags, **quit in an interview**, then reconcile, come back in more extreme gothwear and win their way out of the
  bottom. **A team can quit and un-quit within a leg.**
- **A performance meter**: repeated attempts until the crowd is won; failure loops cost time; **watching another
  team's success teaches you** (everyone copies Spud's style).
- **Sabotage by words**: Emma tells Mickey the air is full of bacteria; panic attack; the Twins lose. Emma feels bad.
- **Curse resolved**: the Goths explain Josée's rock.
- **Crossed wires**: Noah gives up on Emma right as Emma realises she likes him. **Timing between two people is drama.**
- Elimination at the task (Don walks up to the last team still performing). The Twins are proud they lasted.

### Ep 12 · My Way or Zimbabwe (Zimbabwe) · non-elimination (Ice Dancers last)

- **A team spends a leg on a personal errand**: the Ice Dancers fly back to Hawaii to return the cursed rock and do a
  ritual, then race the leg late. **Their story runs in parallel, in another country, intercut with the race.**
- **Driving yourself**: jeeps. MacArthur taunts the Daters, they bicker, drive off-road and **find a shortcut** (second
  to the Don Box). Getting lost can help.
- **Route**: All-In: raft over the falls and take a team selfie mid-drop with a waterproof camera; miss the shot →
  climb back up and redo → All-In: one partner photographs the other with a white rhino → Chill Zone.
- **Teams get separated**: Dwayne and Junior lose each other at the falls; each follows a *different* "family rule"
  (Dwayne: go back to where we last saw each other; Junior: go to the destination). **Junior tags along with the BFFs**,
  they help each other with the rhino; he reaches the mat but **can't check in without his partner** and waits.
  **Separation is a great engine: two stories, one team.**
- **Road rage between teams**: Dwayne asks the Ice Dancers if they've seen Junior; Josée lies, he says something
  offensive, she tries to run him over.
- **Animals as hazards with attitude**: MacArthur insults the rhino, it chases her until it collapses (Sanders gets the
  photo); a rhino throws Dwayne onto the mat; Jacques insults a rhino and the Ice Dancers get chased in last.
- **Last, non-elim, and the turn**: the Ice Dancers, last, get ready for a goodbye; non-elimination; they announce their
  luck has turned and **come back as villains** (next leg: black outfits).

### Ep 13 · Shawshank Ridonc-tion (Australia) · non-elimination (Cadets last)

- **Waiting for the last team**: the shared bus waits for the Ice Dancers (last), who arrive in new black outfits to
  intimidate. **An entrance as a statement.**
- **Twist announced on the plane**: Don explains **Boomerangs**. MacArthur decides immediately whom to hit; Sanders
  wants to think strategically. **Teams plan a twist before it appears.**
- **Reward announced in advance changes motives**: a phone call home for first. Devin wants it for Shelley (Carrie
  worries); Sanders wants to call her sick grandma, MacArthur her cat (flip for it).
- **Route**: All-In: teams are handcuffed and locked in prison cells; escape by one of four methods (tunnels, trap doors,
  tools, bars) → All-In: build a raft and sail down the river to the Chill Zone. **Boomerangs usable only at the river
  Don Box; they expire after.**
- **Discovery inside a task**: the Stepbrothers find the same plan, then common ground (movies, games), then a trapdoor
  by accident. **A task can fix a team.** Their bonding costs them later (they stop to role-play and write a theme song).
- **Deals across teams, broken**: Sanders asks the Ice Dancers to free them; Josée demands MacArthur compliment ice
  dancing; she does; **the Ice Dancers leave them anyway**. The Sisters break into the Pros' cell and **the four escape
  together** through the sewer.
- **Mutual Boomerang**: the Ice Dancers and Cadets aim at each other; agree to rip them both on three; neither does;
  both Boomerang each other → **both sent back to prison, same cell ("the black hole")**, forced to cooperate
  (temporary alliance) to get out. **Revenge that costs both** is the funniest kind.
- **Unrequited thread**: Carrie keeps trying to confess (taxi, cell, finish line); interrupted every time. The
  phone-call reward delivers the twist: **Shelley dumps Devin on the phone**. Carrie holds her confession.
- **Final stretch sabotage**: Josée knocks the Cadets off their raft with an oar → Cadets last → non-elim → they vow
  revenge. **The grudge escalates leg by leg.**

### Ep 14 · Down and Outback (Australia → New Zealand) · DOUBLE elimination (Rockers + Stepbrothers)

- **Route**: All-In: catch ten rabbits on a farm; **the rare albino rabbit skips the task** (a shortcut inside a task)
  → All-In: fly a glider from Australia to the Chill Zone in New Zealand mountains.
- **Character as advantage**: the rabbits love Ennui (the Goths hate it). **Character as obstacle**: the Stepbrothers
  keep chasing a kangaroo they think is the albino and get beaten up.
- **Fake shortcut as sabotage**: Josée paints a rabbit white with Ennui's makeup; MacArthur abandons all her rabbits to
  chase it, wrecks a farm with a tractor, ends in the river. **Then Josée uses Brody's crush to lure the Surfers away and
  steals their rabbits.** The villain arc fully live.
- **Helping costs the helper**: Carrie stays to learn Spud's "seven stages of heartbreak" song for Devin; both teams
  fall behind.
- **Odd luck**: two rabbits give birth (the Daters reach ten); a snake hands Spud rabbits; MacArthur covers herself in
  carrots to collect rabbits for both her team and the Surfers (help among allies).
- **Crashes**: Spud crashes the glider into a mountain; a kangaroo climbs onto the Stepbrothers' glider and crashes it.
  An avalanche delivers the Rockers to the mat **at the same moment** the Stepbrothers arrive. **Don checks the replay.**
- **Double elimination revealed after the first** ("only to remember"). Losers leave friends (video games together).
  **Two teams that bottomed together leave together.**

### Ep 15 · Maori or Less (New Zealand) · non-elimination (Reality TV Pros last)

- **Trains in waves** again: Father & Son miss the first train (Dwayne tired) despite being top four. **The third train
  arriving forces a decision** (Kitty picks bungee the instant she sees it).
- **Route**: **Either/Or**: Jump Down (bungee off a bridge and catch a fish bare-handed) / Jump Around (perform a
  traditional dance, approved by locals) → Chill Zone.
- **Identity skill again**: the Ice Dancers learn the dance in minutes and arrive so early Don isn't camera-ready and
  thinks they skipped it.
- **Mind games inside a team**: Stephanie tricks Ryan into jumping; Ryan reverse-psychologies her into it.
- **Romance costs focus**: Emma and Noah nearly kiss (interrupted by the Surfers); Emma loses focus; **Kitty takes charge
  of the team**; Emma puts the relationship on hold; Noah goes catatonic; Owen cheers him up. Last, non-elim.
- **Cross-team mentoring**: Junior hangs out with the Surfers; Dwayne, lonely, asks Brody for parenting advice; tries
  to act "cool", breakdances instead of the haka; Junior yells; Dwayne channels the anger into a correct dance.
  **A fight between partners can fuel the task.** Devin's breakup anger fuels his haka too.
- **Aftermath gag**: Dwayne gets a tattoo to impress Junior; it's a women's tattoo.

### Ep 16 · Little Bull on the Prairie (Alberta) · Father & Son out

- **Carry-over consequences**: Dwayne is mocked for the tattoo and falls behind getting a cab; Noah, catatonic after
  Emma, is **carried by Owen the whole leg** and Owen drives without a licence (hits animals). **Last leg's emotional
  state is this leg's handicap.**
- **Flight delay by weather**: the second flight waits out a storm and lands **an hour after** the first. Dwayne
  jinxes it on the plane ("content even if we're eliminated").
- **Vehicle sabotage**: the Ice Dancers try to run the Cadets off the road; MacArthur rams them repeatedly; **both go
  off course and the Surfers pass both to the ranch**. Feuds hand the lead to a third team.
- **Route**: pickup trucks to a ranch → All-In: eat a whole pot of pork and beans, the tip is at the bottom (both
  must eat) → **Random Botch**: the partner *not holding* the tip rides a mechanical bull for eight seconds → Chill Zone.
- **All-In rule as drama**: MacArthur hogs the pot; Sanders tricks her into looking away to eat a spoonful (penalty
  avoided). Ryan motivates Stephanie with reverse psychology ("loser").
- **A token of trust fixes a failing task**: Geoff gives Brody his hat; Brody stays on the bull; first. Junior's pep talk

## Part 2: what the 26 episodes add up to

### The shape of a leg

1. **Start** at last leg's Chill Zone, **in finishing order**, staggered (the Don Box at the mat). The gap is real for
   about ten minutes.
2. **Travel**, where the gap mostly evaporates: everyone on the same bus; helicopters that leave once six teams are
   aboard; trains and flights in two or three batches. **Batches are the main structure of the first half of a leg**:
   the first batch races while the next is in the air and the last is waiting at the airport.
3. **Two to four tasks**, chained: an All-In or two, a Botch or Watch most legs, an Either/Or five times a season.
   **Tasks feed each other** (the spices you pick become the stew you eat; the pepper you eat sets the cliff your
   partner jumps).
4. **The last stretch to the mat** is its own scene: wrong ways, footraces, collisions, a sweet moment at the wrong time.
5. **The mat**: Don gives the place, applies penalties (the team waits beside the mat with a clock), and for the last
   team, either eliminates them or fakes them out ("…is what I'd say if this weren't a non-elimination round").

### Where time is won and lost (the race engine needs every one of these)

- **Batches and capacity**: flight seats by arrival order; helicopter waits for six; two teams per sauna; one ledge;
  one elevator. **Capacity turns a lead into a queue, and the queue into scenes.**
- **Equalisers by accident**: a burst tyre on the first plane, a storm delay, narwhals popping planes, a slow-talking
  pilot. Several legs put everyone on one plane.
- **Task loops**: say the phrase wrong, run back; miss the selfie, climb back up; the crowd meter doesn't fill, back of
  the line; the judge rejects the drawing/costume/dance/igloo. **A task is a loop until a judge says yes.**
- **Travel mishaps**: flat tyres, lost drivers, a broken-down boat, a camel that collapses, an elephant on the taxi.
- **Injury and illness**: cramps, sprains, spice blindness, electrocution (carries into the next leg), broken arm.
- **Penalties at the mat** (10–60 min): reading the tip early, skipping the train, one partner skipping an All-In or a
  re-run, swapping roles, cheating, sabotage of course equipment. **About half of all eliminations in the season's
  first third were decided by a penalty clock.**
- **Shortcuts**: a jeep goes off-road and finds a faster way; the albino rabbit; the Zip-It catfish; Jen's compliment
  instead of the phrase. Shortcuts exist and are rare.
- **Choosing**: Either/Or picks and switches, mostly made by **watching other teams** suffer or succeed.

### How the last team is found

- Last onto the mat (most legs), **often a footrace of two or three** in sight of each other.
- **Don goes to them** when they are hopelessly behind: by helicopter at the sandcastle, at the air guitar stage, at the
  rug pile. **The team never reaches the mat**, and the exit happens where they are.
- **Tie on the mat**: decided by a foot off the carpet, or by replay.
- **Double elimination**, revealed after the first ("I forgot").
- **Penalty clock**: a team that arrived earlier is passed while serving it, or serves it out and steps on just as the
  team behind arrives.
- **Medevac**: the injured team leaves and **chooses an eliminated team to return** (and picks the one that was kind to
  them).
- Non-elimination legs: **11 of 26** (most of the early non-elims fall on legs where the last team is a fan favourite).
  The fake-out is a signature beat.

### What teams do to each other

- **Help**: sharing a cab, giving a spare ring, carrying a rival out of danger, sharing a rickshaw, rescuing someone
  from a dragon, finishing a squeegee column for another team, lending a hand at a jump. **Help usually costs the
  helper** (the Surfers end up in the same tree, then let the BFFs pass and go home) and **is repaid later** (the
  Surfers are chosen to return).
- **Deals**: help for an alliance (rickshaw for an alliance; free us from the cell if you compliment ice dancing). Deals
  get broken (the Ice Dancers leave the Cadets in the cell).
- **Alliances**: formal ones (Sisters + Reality TV Pros; Sisters + BFFs), trial ones (Sisters + Twins, dissolved after a
  tie), forced ones (Superteam), villain ones (the Axis: three teams who hate each other agree to hate the rest more,
  each secretly planning betrayal), and **refused** ones (LARPers, Rock, the Goths turning down the BFFs because Carrie
  is too cheerful). Alliance members hand each other placement on the mat and stop short of sacrificing themselves
  ("not in last place, so no help").
- **Sabotage, every kind**:
  - physical: pushing someone downhill, ramming taxis and trucks, an oar to the head, a coconut throw, unhooking train cars;
  - theft: a fossil, rabbits, a ball in the dark, a pet;
  - course sabotage: brakes, oiled emu, a fake albino rabbit;
  - mind games: taunts, telling Geoff that Brody's angry, telling Mickey the air is full of bacteria;
  - using a secret: Spud can't refuse a dare;
  - admin sabotage: seats by the toilets, trying to force the agent to put everyone on a later flight.
- **Sabotage usually costs the saboteur**: both feuding teams fall behind and a third team wins; the stolen fossil is
  refused; Loki escapes and scatters their burros (penalty); mind games make the Surfers stronger; course sabotage is
  penalised. **The villains still win a lot of legs**, and lose the race at the end. The audience is promised
  comeuppance through victims' exits.
- **Boomerang**: decided by who you hate (MacArthur knows instantly), traded threats, a broken promise to rip both,
  self-Boomerang out of spite, waiting at the box for a victim, other teams guessing who did it.

### What happens inside a team

- **Leadership shifts** on events: Junior takes over when Dwayne is poisoned; Kitty takes charge when Emma is lovesick;
  Carrie when Devin keeps hurting her; Sanders after MacArthur breaks her arm ("only if you listen to me now").
- **Managing your partner**: bribes (snacks), reverse psychology ("loser"), distraction (talking about cold things),
  pep talks, a lucky hat, covering a partner's mouth.
- **Fights cost time and are about something real**: who founded the blog; bought trophies; an Olympic drop; being
  treated like a kid. **Fixing the fight fixes the task** (Fashion Bloggers, Stepbrothers, Surfers, Father & Son).
- **Spite costs time**: Stephanie eats the mild pepper so Ryan jumps the highest cliff; Ryan hits every bump.
- **Quitting** happens in an interview and can be undone in the same leg (the Goths).
- **Separation**: Dwayne and Junior lose each other and follow different family rules; the mat needs both.
- **Romance**: within teams (the Daters' breakup at the mat, the silent treatment, getting back together and losing
  because of it) and **across teams** (Noah and Emma, Brody and MacArthur, Junior's crush on Carrie, Carrie holding
  Ryan's hand → Devin's jealousy). Romance costs focus and places, and is **carried by third parties** (Kitty's love
  radar, Owen and Kitty setting up Noah and Emma, the Sisters telling Devin).

### Season arcs it built from small things

- A rock taken from a beach → a curse → three bad legs → a side trip to return it → a villain turn.
- A crush kept secret → a girlfriend on the phone → the stages of heartbreak, one per leg → the roles reverse → a kiss
  that thinks it's last place → a medevac.
- A team that lets another pass → eliminated → chosen to return → wins the race (US ending).
- The finale calls back the first episode: the same stairs, the same cramp, a banana in the pocket this time.

### The writing (from Wikiquote and the plots)

- **Don**: opens every leg with "**X! Home of A, B, and something that annoys me.**" Explains each task at the location
  with a small gag (a shark lunges, a crab pinches). Dry and self-absorbed: "**Don't care.**" ×3, "**No sharing.**",
  "**Kidding.**", "Sweet sister of ducks", the fake-out ("is what I'd say if this were an elimination round"), "TV
  fishing" (gives them hope, then yanks it). He hates themed Don Boxes and likes one. He's openly bored by some teams and
  scared of others.
- **Tips have pun titles**, read aloud by whoever pulls the tip: "The spice is right", "Who's ready to go deep?",
  "How to Milk Your Dragon", "Tip Your Hat, Split Your Jeans…", "Who Loves Peppers… and Who Loves Dip?". Reading the
  tip aloud is how the viewer learns the task, with Don's demo after.
- **"It's a Botch or Watch. Whose turn is it?" / "Yours."** The alternation is tracked on screen by the teams.
- **Interviews are both partners together**, short, and they **contradict each other** ("I'm not too competitive."
  "Yes, you are."), give one piece of backstory (how they met, a childhood story), or say the feeling the race hides
  (Carrie on Devin; Josée's tantrum). Solo interviews happen for secrets.
- **Exits are warm**: what they got from it, a small joke, a hug. Even the villains' exit ends on a gag.
- Characters each have **one strong verbal habit**: Brody's "dude"/bro talk, MacArthur's "Hustle!" and threats,
  Ennui's flat "Yeah."/"Ow.", Crimson's deadpan, Josée's medal obsession (silver, bronze, "there is always a podium"),
  Stephanie's escalation, Ryan's "Yes, Stephanie", Spud's delayed reactions, Laurie's causes, Dwayne's dad-jokes and
  coupons, Taylor's "totes".
- **For this project**: the show's comedy is quippy, and the user has ruled out clever lines in the stepped viewer.
  What carries over is the situation comedy, the habits and the contradictions between partners, said in plain speech.

## Part 3: The Amazing Race

Read: the Amazing Race wiki's rules page and every format page (Detour, Roadblock, Fast Forward, Yield, U-Turn,
Intersection, Speed Bump, Switchback, Express Pass, Hazard, Save, Double Battle/Head-to-Head, Starting Line Task,
Scramble, City Sprint, Partner Swap, Superleg, Megaleg, No-Rest Leg, Overnight Rest, Pit Start, Pit Stop, Money,
Non-Elimination Leg and all its penalties, Elimination, Surprise and Double Elimination, Quit, Evacuation, Final Memory
Challenge, Finish Line), and **every leg of four seasons** (5, 7, 14 and 25: departure times, routes, every Detour and
Roadblock, who chose what, results), plus the cast labels. The wiki and Wikipedia record routes, not what happened
between people; **the Amazing Race gives us the rules and the structure, the Ridonculous Race gives us the social layer.**

### The rules that shape a leg

- **Pit Start**: teams leave in arrival order, and **the gap is real**: departure = arrival + the rest period, so a team
  that checked in 40 minutes behind leaves 40 minutes behind. Seen gaps: one minute, two hours, **seven and a half hours**
  (a Fast Forward in season 5). Each leg starts with a cash envelope (sometimes nothing; once $1).
- **Money** matters: taxis, buses, food, tickets for some transport. Zero-money legs; non-elimination penalties that take
  all of it (and in season 7 all possessions too). Teams beg (not at airports), sell what they bought, borrow from other
  teams. In season 7 the team that had to surrender all their money and possessions on the second-to-last leg won the
  final one.
- **Flights**: economy only, booked by the team with the show's card; one itinerary per team (from season 25). Production
  often fixes the choice: two flights, the first carries six teams, the second the rest; **a later flight can land first**
  because of a layover. Charter flights with sign-up sheets; tickets hidden in sand or on trees for set departure times
  the next morning; departure tokens (tambourines, Maltese crosses) with times on them.
- **Equalisers**: places that open at a set hour (an overnight rest where everyone catches up), sign-up departure times,
  shuttles and trucks with fixed capacity (three teams per truck, two teams per seaplane, four workstations), charter
  planes that wait. **Every leg has at least one moment where the gap resets or shrinks.**
- **The 20-foot rule**: partners stay together; neither may open a clue without the other; teams must stop to read a
  clue. Teams may lie and mislead; they may work together; **they may not hinder another team** except through the
  Yield or U-Turn, take another team's car or clue, or touch another team's things.
- **Detour**: two tasks, one usually physical/fast, one tedious/slow-but-sure. Switching is free except for the time. In
  practice the choice is **often nearly unanimous** (one side is plainly better), and the outliers are character.
  Blind Detour: you only know the names.
- **Roadblock**: a **vague teaser** ("Who's got the sweet tooth?", "Who's not a vegetarian?", "Who has a good memory for
  faces?"); the team picks who **before** learning the task, and can't switch. The other partner may only cheer. **Max six
  Roadblocks per person** in a season. **Quitting costs four hours** (eating tasks are where people quit: three quit a
  four-pound meat feast; three quit a beach dig).
- **Fast Forward**: one team only, skips everything to the Pit Stop; at most one per team per season; often something
  you would rather not do (shaving your head); a team that goes for it and gets beaten has wasted the time.
- **Yield** (hourglass, about 30 min), **U-Turn** (do the other Detour side too; Blind U-Turn hides who did it; Double
  U-Turn has two slots), **Intersection** (pair with another team until told otherwise; wait for one if alone),
  **Speed Bump** (the last team on a non-elimination leg does an extra task next leg), **Express Pass** (skip one task;
  earned by winning a leg or an optional search; a second one must be given to another team), **Save** (survive one
  last-place finish), **Switchback** (a famous past task returns), **Head-to-Head** (a queue of duels; the loser waits for
  the next team; the last one is penalised or out), **Starting Line Task** with a penalty for last (U-Turn, Hazard).
- **Twist usage seen**: Yields mostly unused for ten legs, then used at the end on the dominant team (Chip & Kim on
  Colin & Christie) or for revenge (Rob & Amber on Ron & Kelly). U-Turns on a rival team (Margie & Luke on Amanda & Kris,
  who went out; Tammy & Victor on Kisha & Jen in the semi-final; Shelley & Nici on Keith & Whitney, who went out). **A
  U-Turn can be wasted**: the target already passed it, or used the Fast Forward.
- **Penalties**: 30 minutes standard (+ any time gained for wrong transport), 15 for minor equipment misuse, 4 h to quit
  a Roadblock, 6 h to skip a Detour, 2 h if weather or opening hours made a task impossible, 24 h for flying above
  economy, 30 min for speeding. Served at the mat ("you are the fourth team to arrive… however"). **A last-place team's
  penalty isn't applied on an elimination leg**; on a non-elimination leg it carries into the next start.
- **Non-elimination legs**: predetermined and unannounced ("the last team to arrive *may* be eliminated"). Penalties by
  era: none; surrender all money; surrender money and possessions; Marked for Elimination (30 min unless you win the next
  leg); Speed Bump; departure-time penalty.
- **Eliminations**: last onto the mat; the host goes **to the team** if they can't or won't finish; quits (injury); medical
  evacuations; surprise eliminations mid-leg (last to a midpoint marker, or a Starting Line Task); double eliminations; a
  **midpoint elimination in the final leg**.
- **Double-length legs**: Superleg (the host meets them on the mat and hands them a clue: "you're still racing"), Megaleg,
  No-Rest Leg (check in, get the next clue on the spot).
- **The finale**: a **memory task** about the season itself (place eleven surfboards, find containers marked with every city
  in order, a totem of the countries' animals), then the Finish Line with **all eliminated teams waiting**.
- **Rewards**: a trip, a car, cash, every leg. (The Ridonculous Race made them small and rare.)

### What the four seasons show

- **The shape of a leg**: one flight or long ground trip, then 4–8 markers: almost always one Detour and one Roadblock, plus
  short Route Info tasks (carry 55 lb of beef half a mile; find a gnome and carry it; find a dancer from a photo), then the
  Pit Stop. Signature small tasks give a place its flavour as much as the big ones.
- **The dominant team rarely wins**: Colin & Christie won six legs and finished second; Rob & Amber won four and finished
  second to a team that had lost all their money on the leg before. **The race is won on the last leg.**
- **Tasks loop until a local approves**: the wrong dancer takes your programme and you queue again; the pancake falls and you
  cook again; the go-kart lap is too slow and you wait 20 minutes; the wrong drawer sends you to the back of the line.
- **Choices with information**: wait for the wind to paraglide, or hike an hour down. Teams pick looking at what's in front
  of them.
- **Cast labels are relationship + identity**, exactly what the identities doc proposes: Dating Models, Bowling Moms, Military
  Dad and Daughter, Former POW/Pageant Queen, Siblings/Lawyers, Brothers/Stuntmen, Former NFL Cheerleaders, Flight
  Attendants, Married Dentists, Urban Bike Racers, Dating Pro Wrestlers, Food Scientists, Realtor Sisters, Married Surfers,
  Firefighters. And **relationship states**, not just types: Once Engaged, Dating On and Off, Newly Dating, Internet Dating
  Couple, College Sweethearts.

### Where the two shows differ, and what each gives the merge

| | Ridonculous Race | Amazing Race | For us |
