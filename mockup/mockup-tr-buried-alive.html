Mission · Episode Six

Buried Alive

Six of you go into the ground. The rest have forty-five minutes, nine plots and a spade each to work out which three are theirs.

Three teams · Yew · Rook · Lych 45 minutes £2,500 a person out before the bell One Shield, sealed inside a coffin

The Briefing

A churchyard at dusk. Nine freshly turned plots behind the lych gate, six coffins on trestles beside them, a pile of spades, and a bell hanging in the gate.

The host waits at the lych gate with one hand on the bell rope while the players file in past six open coffins.

Three teams. Two of each team are going into these coffins, and the coffins are going into the ground.

There are nine plots behind me and only six of them have anybody in. Three of the others hold a clue, and the clue tells you which plot your own people are under. Dig a clue plot first. Dig the wrong plot and you have spent the time it took to dig it.

The host lifts a spade off the pile and plants it in the turf.

When your spade hits a lid, knock twice. If the person inside knocks back, get the ropes on and bring them up. You lift together, or you drop them, and a dropped coffin goes back to the bottom of the hole.

The host turns one of the empty lids over. A sealed envelope is nailed to the underside.

Every lid has one of these. Inside is a riddle, and a lamp to read it by. Whoever solves theirs first has won a Shield.

Here is the catch. When your team knocks, you can knock back, or you can tell them "not yet." Every minute you stay down is a minute your team is not digging for somebody else.

Every one of you standing on the grass when this bell rings is two and a half thousand pounds in the pot. Anybody still underground earns nothing. Forty-five minutes. Dig.

The host rings the bell once.
