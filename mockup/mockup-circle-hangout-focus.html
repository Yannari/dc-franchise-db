The Hangout: a profile opens, the verdict lands

When the Influencers start on someone, that player's profile opens full-screen on the LED wall: photo, name, age, status, job, bio, with a pulsing DISCUSSING tag and the other at-risk faces in a strip below. The verdict stamps down on the line where they decide, never before. Then the profile shrinks back into its tile on the board, which keeps the mark. Click the stage or "Next" to step through it.
