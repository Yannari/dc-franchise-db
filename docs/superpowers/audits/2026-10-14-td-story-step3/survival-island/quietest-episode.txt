=== VOTING PLANS — GOPHERS ===
VOTING MATH:
  8 attending - 0 without a vote = 8 eligible voters.
  5 ballots are needed to guarantee majority control. A read of "5 supporting; 5 needed" means the plan is one ballot short of controlling the vote.
  A target does NOT need 5 votes to leave. If the field splits, the highest valid total wins by plurality; 3-2-2-2-1 still eliminates the player with 3.
  Players without votes can still discuss strategy and influence allies, but contribute zero ballots. Extra Votes add ballots only when played at Tribal.

ALLIANCE PLANS:
BALLOT RELIABILITY DICTIONARY:
