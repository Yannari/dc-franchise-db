Bloodtide

⚡ The Turn Sequence
Pre-combat:

Oath of Desecration on Fane (+10% damage)
Arcane Volley to strip Magic Armour on priority targets

Turn 1 (with Time Warp):

Raining Blood (1 AP) — Blood everywhere, Decaying on enemies
Death Wish (2 AP) — Self-buff active
Blood Barrage (3 AP) — Sacrifice 15% HP, massive damage hits

Time Warp Turn:
4. Shackles of Pain (1 AP) — Bind to tankiest enemy
5. Sanguine Torrent (3 AP) — Your remaining HP means massive Death Wish bonus; Shackles mirrors your sacrifice to your bound target
6. Mystic Rupture (1 AP) — Debuff a surviving high-priority target

The 1-HP Reaper Finisher:

Cast Living on the Edge before going low
Ride sacrifice spells down to 1 HP for maximum Death Wish
Play Dead (Fane racial) if in danger — wait out the Decaying DoTs
Blood Sucker next turn on a Blood surface → full HP reset, repeat
