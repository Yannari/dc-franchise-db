1. A QUEER VERSION
------------------

What the real shows did
  - Love Island UK: never a queer season. One same-sex couple in twelve series
    (Sophie and Katie, 2016). Producers have said the format's coupling rules
    don't fit a mixed villa.
  - I Kissed a Boy (BBC Three, 2023) / I Kissed a Girl (2024), host Dannii
    Minogue: an all-gay-men or all-queer-women villa. Islanders couple with a
    kiss the moment they meet; the rest runs close to Love Island.
  - Are You The One? season 8 (2019): everyone sexually fluid, anyone can
    match anyone. The hardest season of that show to solve, because the
    possible pairings explode.

The idea
  Give each islander a "who they're into" field in cast setup: men / women /
  everyone. Blank = the current straight rules, so existing seasons do not
  change. The two real-show styles then fall out as presets:
    - one-gender villa (I Kissed a Boy / Girl)
    - everyone into everyone (AYTO 8)

What would have to change
  - Who can couple with whom: attraction, the first coupling and every
    recoupling follow each islander's preference, not gender. Coupling stops
    being boy-girl pairs and becomes a general matching.
  - Who picks at a recoupling: "the girls pick" means nothing in an all-boys
    villa, so picking goes by turn or by popularity.
  - Bombshells: balanced today by gender; would be balanced by who the villa
    is short of for the people already in it.
  - Dumping formats: cross-gender only works in a mixed villa, so it drops out
    elsewhere.
  - Lines and the host: every "the boys" / "the girls", and the gendered word
    slots ({~Girl} etc.), need an all-one-gender reading.
  - Eyes-on: could finally get a die on the Villa card, because it would know
    who each islander actually likes.
  - Step Forward (night one) assumes one side steps forward for the other;
    needs a one-gender version.

Before building
  Read the real shows first (I Kissed a Boy episode structure especially),
  then write a spec. Build the per-islander field first; both presets come
  out of it.
