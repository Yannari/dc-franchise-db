# Secret Story (France): every twist, season by season, S1–S14

The user's catalogue, 2026-10-28 (sources: tele-realite.fandom, fr/en Wikipedia, Puremédias, RTL, TF1+).
Not every daily mission or single dilemma is listed: no public index of those exists. This is the reference
for the Secret Story twist engine (part 5).

**Built already (2026-10-28):** the red phone; dilemmas (including the Bridge of Sacrifices' trades); the
secret room (a fake elimination into the attic); the Masters of the Nominations; the House Thieves; the
black buzzer; the house secret and the final ticket; mini-secrets and the Oops night; Miss and Mister
Secret; house missions; one clan war a season. The S1–S7 production secrets (fake couple, fake siblings,
the Voice's accomplice and the others) exist as secrets, without their own gameplay yet.

## The list

**Season 1 (2007)**
- The triplets' identity swap: Marjorie, Johanna and Cyrielle alternated as one contestant.
- A secret married couple: Xavier and Tatiana.
- The Voice's accomplice: Gabriel.
- A fake double departure: Xavier and Maryline spent two days in a secret room, watching.
- The red telephone: money, penalties, changed nominations.
- Transferable immunity: Tatiana gave hers to a man, Xavier.
- Double nomination votes: Ophélie won one by the phone; Gabriel won one for protecting his secret.

**Season 2 (2008)**
- Mother and daughter together: Marie-France and Maëva.
- A public referendum: after Marie-France revealed it and was disqualified, viewers decided whether Maëva stayed.
- A delayed partner entrance: Nicolas came in after Caroline.
- A fake double eviction: Alice and Cyril spent a week in a secret bedroom, then were immune.
- The white confrontation room: meetings with outsiders and former contestants.
- Nomination penalties for exposing a shared secret.

**Season 3 (2009)**
- The House of Intruders: Angie, Romain, Kévin and Léo had a separate base, surveillance and temporary immunity, and were exchanged between the houses.
- A disguised celebrity guest: "Rosa".
- A late contestant.
- The "Seventh Heaven" hidden garden room.
- A real eviction combined with fake departures: Romain left, while Émilie and Jonathan went to a secret room.
- Reverse surveillance: the house was told about the hideout and could deceive the spies.
- The housemates voted on whether an excluded player came back.
- Outgoing-contestant immunity.
- Exclusive nomination powers: first Didier, then Vanessa.
- Money-versus-immunity dilemmas: €10,000 refused for immunity; immunity at the house's cost.

**Season 4 (2010)**
- The internet-selected fake couple: Benoît and Robin.
- The antechamber: Amélie, Maxime and Chrismaëlle started apart.
- The Chosen Ones: Ahmed, Julie and Anne-Krystel. The antechamber had to hunt for them, with their places at stake.
- The predictions room: privileged information.
- The Corridor of Secrets: clues.
- A fake pregnancy and a secret isolation: Bastien and Anne-Krystel.
- The Joker card: cancel a nomination and put somebody else up.
- A collective bank: pooled kitties, with money decisions that touched the nominations.
- A second house that split couples and alliances.
- The clock puzzle and the golden buzzer: the house mystery's place in the final, which Stéphanie gave to Bastien.
- A robot with a hidden identity: "Monsieur Robot".

**Season 5 (2011)**
- The masters of the underground passages: Zarko and Zelko used tunnels to listen in.
- Nomination for life: the twins condemned one contestant to every nomination, and puzzles and powers later moved it.
- Swapped couples, hiding their real pairs.
- Another fake couple: Ayem and Daniel.
- A public entry vote: Anthony or Élisabeth.
- The Bridge of Sacrifices: money, clues, immunity and nominations traded through dilemmas.
- A fake telephone eviction: Juliette went to the parallel room, with nomination control.
- A fake eviction for Geoffrey while Morgan really left.
- Immunity House versus Nomination House.
- The four-elements puzzle: immunity.
- The Joker card and the inverted-nomination card.
- Aurélie's fake elimination by Juliette, into a secret room as nomination master.
- The house-secret ticket to the final, given away after a dilemma.

**Season 6 (2012)**
- The Secret Box: Audrey, Midou and Émilie hid in a cube in the living room, with an entry challenge of finding secrets.
- Fake siblings, arriving late: Nadège and Thomas.
- Linked nominations: nominate one, and both are in danger.
- The Library of Secrets: books that grant powers, including immunity.
- The Trust Show: immunity or automatic nomination.
- A Joker intervention: save one, nominate another.
- A game-master wish: a nomination.
- Immunity House versus Nomination House, decided by duels.
- The inverted-nominations card, and a public vote on the one who broke it.
- A fake voluntary departure: Kévin joined Virginie.
- The red nomination room: whoever enters it is nominated, and others are drawn in.
- Julien as the access code: the house mystery, and a four-person nomination power.
- A secret public vote on a pair, whose "eviction" sent them to a secret room, from where they chose the nominees.
- A decision on a place in the final.

**Season 7 (2013)**
- The Before Secret: viewers shaped a shared cover story online.
- The public's fake family: the Vanderbecks.
- The common secret: Gautier had met everybody when handing over their passes.
- Secret nomination masters: Tara and Vincent.
- The secret elevator: hidden spaces, encounters and dilemmas.
- An opening public vote on the whole house.
- A fake double eviction to the secret apartment, from where the pair could replace nominees in secret.
- Immunity House versus Nomination House, and a decision that could swap which house was immune.
- The Labyrinth of Secrets: a dark hidden challenge.
- Final tickets that could be surrendered or destroyed.
- A fake cash-out departure into another secret operation.
- Returning contestants as accomplices, running nomination and relationship deceptions.
- The final house puzzle: a place in the final.

**Season 8 (2014)**
- The Secret Zone: four started under the house, as impostors who had to persuade the house to let them in, and pretended to be eight.
- A marriage arranged for the game, in Las Vegas.
- A couple pretending to be mother and son.
- A stay-or-leave public vote.
- A fake immunity bracelet.
- A second-house division.
- The housemates chose who left after a public vote.
- A fake eviction, with an accomplice in the house told; remote-control missions from the secret room.
- Immunity-or-nomination envelopes, and sacrifices.

**Season 9 (2015)**
- The immunity robbery: one group stole the protection, and the defenders were nominated.
- The Voice's adventurers: two started abroad, with powers to grant immunity and to block a nomination vote.
- A contestant chosen by the public.
- A returning contestant under another identity.
- A couple created by the production "as an experiment".
- An accomplice protecting twins.
