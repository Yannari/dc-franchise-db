I. The SummonsFour names are read out. None of them were told why.
II. The DealEight cards between them. One of the eight lets you leave.
III. First RoundAnne Maria turns over the life card and walks out of the game.
IV. Second RoundCameron finds the second one. Two chairs left, and eight cards.
V. The CircleAll eight go back face down, and they draw in turn.
VI. The Last CardB turns it over with 3 cards still to go. Brightly does not.
VII. Face To FaceThe chair belongs to one of their own. They have to do it anyway, and in front of everybody.
