The display face

The previous pass was briefed to find a Didone and picked Bodoni Moda. That brief was wrong. The logotype's serifs are wedges — they flare out of the stem and end in a point, a Roman/Latin trait. A Didone's serifs are flat, unbracketed slabs. Bodoni is not a near miss; it is the wrong genus.

What was actually wrong with the first draft's Cinzel was weight and width. So Cinzel is not disqualified — it is under-tested. The heaviest weight it ships, 900, had never been put on the page.

Every specimen below is normalised to the logotype's cap height by measurement, not by eye. The logo strip is sticky: it stays on screen while the candidates scroll past it.
