=== VOTING PLANS ===
Julia is safe (Demolition Derby challenge). Everyone else is vulnerable.
VOTING MATH:
  10 attending - 0 without a vote = 10 eligible voters.
  6 ballots are needed to guarantee majority control. A read of "5 supporting; 6 needed" means the plan is one ballot short of controlling the vote.
  A target does NOT need 6 votes to leave. If the field splits, the highest valid total wins by plurality; 3-2-2-2-1 still eliminates the player with 3.
