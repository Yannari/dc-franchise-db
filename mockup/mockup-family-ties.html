A family tie is a fact about two people, not about one season. Set it once on the character, and every show, every season, Dramagram, life after the show and the wiki know it. Mockup only: nothing here is wired yet.

Life after the show

it starts where the tie says
Rosa and Luis: married
The starting point, from the character. Not something that "happened".
The Circle, Season 3
Rosa and Mateo play one profile. Luis watches from home.
Off-season
The couple can still have their story: they renew their vows, or it gets rocky. A divorce always asks you first.

A married couple set on the character starts the life layer married. Blood ties never change. A couple's "Together" tie is only where they start, and life can move it.
