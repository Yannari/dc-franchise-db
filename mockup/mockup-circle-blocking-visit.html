The blocking and the visit, rebuilt

The night as the real show cuts it: the Hangout keeps the name secret, the announcement drags it out one message at a time, a flashback shows why, then the walk through a bright hallway, everyone waiting in their own apartment, and the door. Mockup: still frames of what each screen looks like at one click.
