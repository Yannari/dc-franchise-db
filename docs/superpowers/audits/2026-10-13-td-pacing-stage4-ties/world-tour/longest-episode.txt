=== THE ROOM — AND WHAT LINGERS ===
There was no single current before Tribal. Aubrey drew the strongest pull at 4, Gwen remained alive at 2, and Heather still had 1. Everyone could see movement; almost nobody could see the whole shape.
Heather's exit exposed how many people had been reading a different room. Courtney and Gabby now have a reason to treat the next confident vote count as a claim to test, not a fact to inherit.
