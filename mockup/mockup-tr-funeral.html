The Briefing

Alan Cumming · your host

A long avenue of yews. A glass hearse drawn by black horses, an empty carriage behind it, and at the far end, beside an open grave, five coffins on trestles.

Alan Cumming stands beside the hearse in a black coat.

Five of your friends were not at breakfast. One of them was murdered last night. The other four are alive, and waiting.

As we walk, I will read you clues. Each one describes somebody who is safe. Work out who, and they will join us in the carriage.

Alan Cumming walks to the head of the procession.

Get a clue wrong, and whoever it described stays in their coffin with the dead.

At the grave, each of you will lay a lily on the coffin you believe holds the murdered. If most of you are right, the money is yours.

The first lily laid on the right coffin earns a Shield.

Lay it too quickly, and the others may wonder how you were so sure.

Walk slowly. Begin.
